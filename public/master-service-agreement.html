Master Services Agreement (MSA)

 

Rider participation, asset use and ad display agreement

 

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") shall take effect on the date is executed (the "Effective Date") by you - an individual, self-employed independent contractor, who uses a bicycle / e-bike / scooter / motorbike / car, or similar vehicle for deliveries (the "Service Provider"), and will remain in full force and effect between Evox Media Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15604589 and registered office at 7 Bell Yard, London, England, WC2A 2JR, which operates an advertising platform using customised delivery boxes/bags that display digital (still/motion) ads (the "Company"), and you until it is terminated in accordance with Section 3. The Company and the Service Provider are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

 

The Service Provider will provide ad-carriage and related services using the Company's equipment, and the Company will pay the Service Provider for eligible service days and bonuses as set out in applicable statements of work executed under this Agreement. Specific engagements will be documented under one or more statements of work (each, an "SOW" and collectively, "SOWs"), which upon execution form part of this Agreement.

 

1.     Scope of Services (MSA-level, General).  The Service Provider agrees, on a non-exclusive, self-employed independent contractor basis, to perform ad-carriage services and related activities using the Company's equipment during agreed campaign periods as further described in applicable SOWs (the "Services").

(a)   The Service Provider will carry the Company's advertising boxes/bags and related hardware, including without limitation any displays, batteries, chargers, frames, mounts, decals, and other accessories (collectively, the "Equipment") on the Service Provider's vehicle(s) during agreed campaign periods.

(b)   The Service Provider will operate the Equipment safely, keep it charged daily, and comply with the Company's written policies, reasonable instructions, and documentation regarding placement, safe mounting, charging, care, and display quality.

(c)   The Service Provider will permit the collection and use by the Company of GPS/location and telemetric/performance data during service periods for campaign verification, safety, and performance analytics, subject to Section 11 and Exhibit C.

(d)   The Service Provider will follow all applicable road traffic laws and regulations at all times. The Company is not responsible for any traffic fines, penalties, vehicle violations, or the Service Provider's compliance with any vehicle-related laws or requirements.

(e)   The Service Provider will report any loss, theft, or damage to Equipment within 24 hours of becoming aware and will reasonably cooperate in inspections, repairs, replacements, and claims processes.

(f)    Specific campaigns, service windows, performance metrics, and payment terms will be defined in SOWs, which, when executed, become part of this Agreement.

 

2.     Payment Terms.  The Company will pay the Service Provider for Services as defined in each SOW, including rates, bonuses, eligibility criteria, deductions, and timing, and as otherwise set forth in this Section 2.

(a)   Method and currency. The Company will pay the Service Provider directly in pounds sterling (GBP) to the Service Provider's nominated account.

(b)   Timing. Unless stated otherwise in an SOW, the Company will pay amounts due within 15 working days after the end of each calendar month for the prior month's Services.

(c)   Taxes and status. The Service Provider is solely responsible for all taxes, National Insurance contributions, and any required registrations or filings (including self-assessment) arising in connection with the Services and payments hereunder. Nothing in this Agreement creates any employment, worker, agency, or partnership relationship.

(d)   Set-off. The Company may set off or withhold from any amounts payable to the Service Provider any amounts properly due and payable from the Service Provider to the Company under this Agreement or any SOW, including any authorised deductions or amounts due in respect of deposits under Exhibit B.

(e)   Late payment. No contractual late payment penalty applies to the Company beyond any statutory interest to the extent applicable by law. The Company will use reasonable efforts to meet the payment timelines stated in this Section 2 or in the applicable SOW.

 

3.     Term and Termination (MSA-level).

3.1. Term.  This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 3.

3.2. Termination of MSA.

(a)   Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

(b)   Immediate termination by Company. The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice to the Service Provider if the Service Provider: (i) breaches applicable law or safety rules in connection with the Services; (ii) loses or fails to maintain valid right-to-work status; (iii) fails to maintain required insurance; (iv) uses Company’s equipment for illegal activities or to transport animals, explosives or corrosive substances or any other prohibited goods or products; (v) commits fraud or makes a material misrepresentation; (vi) loses, steals, misuses, or fails to safeguard the Company's assets; (vii) operates unlawfully or unsafely; (viii) commits repeated material breaches of this Agreement, an SOW, or the Company's safety/fitment guidelines; (ix) fails to return the Company's assets when due; or (x) causes material reputational harm to the Company or any of its Companies.

(c)   Suspension/termination for inactivity. The Company may suspend or terminate the Service Provider's membership/account if the Service Provider remains offline or declines participation such that the Service Provider performs no campaign days for 30 consecutive days or in two consecutive months.

3.3. Effect of Termination.  Upon termination or cancellation of this Agreement, the Service Provider must return all Equipment and other assets of the Company within 7 working days. The Company will assess the returned Equipment within a further 7 business days and, subject to Exhibit B, refund any deposit balance within 30 days after cancellation, less allowable deductions per Exhibit B.

3.4. SOW Termination.  Each SOW will contain its own suspension/termination terms (including for non-performance, safety issues, and failure to meet eligibility criteria). Termination of an SOW does not automatically terminate this Agreement.

16.1. Definitions. In this clause 16:

(a)   Restricted Period means the term of this Agreement and a period of twelve (12) months from the date of expiry or termination of this Agreement for any reason.

(b)   Relevant Individual means any employee, worker, rider, driver or individual contractor of the Company with whom the Service Provider had material contact in connection with this Agreement during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the start of the Restricted Period or at any time during the Restricted Period.

(c)   Relevant Client means any client, advertiser or other customer of The Company with whom the Service Provider had material contact in connection with this Agreement during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the start of the Restricted Period or at any time during the Restricted Period.

16.2. Non-solicitation of Riders / Drivers. During the Restricted Period, the Service Provider shall not, and shall procure that its group companies and any person acting on its behalf shall not, directly or indirectly solicit, induce or entice away (or attempt to solicit, induce or entice away) any Relevant Individual who is engaged by The Company primarily as a rider or driver, for the purpose of that individual:

(a)   ceasing to provide services to The Company; and/or

(b)   providing advertising carriage or similar services directly to the Service Provider or to any third party introduced by the Service Provider, in each case in a manner which has the object or effect of circumventing The Company

16.3. Non-solicitation of Employees and Contractors. During the Restricted Period, the Service Provider shall not, without Company’s prior written consent, directly or indirectly solicit, induce or entice away (or attempt to solicit, induce or entice away) any Relevant Individual (other than those covered by clause 16.2) for the purpose of employing or engaging such person in a role that is the same as or substantially similar to the role they performed for The Company.

16.4 Non‑circumvention of Clients. During the Restricted Period, the Service Provider shall not, directly or indirectly:

(a)   solicit or accept business from any Relevant Client; or

(b)   enter into any agreement or arrangement (whether directly or via an affiliate or intermediary) with any Relevant Client,

(c)   in each case for the provision of services which are the same as or substantially similar to the services provided by The Company under this Agreement, where such conduct has the object or effect of circumventing The Company or depriving The Company of the benefit of this Agreement.

 

16.5 Carve‑outs. Nothing in this clause 16 shall prevent the Service Provider from:

(a)   placing genuine, general recruitment advertisements that are not targeted at any specific Relevant Individual or class of Relevant Individuals; or

(b)   doing business with any client where the Service Provider can demonstrate, with written evidence, that: (i) such client was an existing customer of the Service Provider prior to the Effective Date; and (ii) the Service Provider’s relationship with such client did not arise, directly or indirectly, from this Agreement or from any introduction by The Company.

16.6 Pre‑existing Riders / Drivers of PCO or Fleet Providers. For the avoidance of doubt, the restrictions in clause 16.2 shall not apply where the Service Provider can demonstrate, with written evidence, that a rider or driver:

(a)   was already directly engaged by the Service Provider (or its group company) before such individual first provided any services in connection with this Agreement; and

(b)   first provided services to The Company as a result of the Service Provider’s existing relationship with that individual, and not as a result of The Company’s separate recruitment, onboarding or marketing activities.

16.7 Reasonableness and Severance. The Service Provider acknowledges that the restrictions in this clause 16 are reasonable and necessary to protect The Company’s legitimate business interests, including its investment in recruiting, onboarding and equipping riders and drivers, and in developing and maintaining client relationships. If any restriction in this clause 16 is held to be invalid or unenforceable but would be valid if part of the wording were deleted, or the period or scope of the restriction were reduced, such restriction shall apply with such modification as is necessary to make it valid and enforceable.

Exhibit E: Safety and Fitment Guidelines

 

 

1.     Approved Mounting Methods/Positions.

(a)   Bicycle/e-bike: If on rear rack or seatpost mount use approved brackets; ensure clearance from wheel, brakes, and reflectors.

(b)   Scooter/motorbike: Rear rack/top box mount with approved vibration-damping brackets; ensure no interference with indicators, brake lights, or license plate.

(c)   Car: Roof rack or rear mount only if approved; no obstruction of rear lights, plate, or driver's field of view.

(d)   General: Follow the Company's mounting diagrams and torque specifications; do not drill or modify frames or vehicle structures; use only approved fasteners.

 

2.     Battery Charging/Handling and Daily Pre-Ride Checks.
